Activity is not direction

Volume measures reported transactions in the provider's units. Every trade has two sides; candle direction does not identify the aggressor. More volume can accompany a breakout, a battle at resistance, or a turn. Compare effort, range, close, location and subsequent completed bars.

ColorMeaningInterpretation
Sky blueClimaxVolume × high-low reaches a 16-bar maximum. At initiation it can mean acceleration; after an extended move it can mean exhaustion.
YellowChurnVolume ÷ high-low reaches a 16-bar maximum. Much activity per price unit suggests a stall; the completed close and location determine the interpretation.
MagentaClimax + churnBoth measurements reach their 16-bar maxima. High effort can coexist with wide range; this alone does not select direction.
Blue / orange / violetWeak comparisonSeven-bar maximum with a recorded strong comparison within 16 bars. It is a shorter-window comparison, not a lower probability trade.
Dark green / dark redHigher / lowerActivity changed relative to the previous bar. Green does not mean buy volume and red does not mean sell volume.
White, disabled by defaultLow participationLowest of three, below 80% of the SMA. Drying participation needs a subsequent price reaction to become directional.

Spikes: blue × minor, dark-magenta triangle major, yellow diamond extreme. Spikes use the documented standard-deviation branch. Extreme high references are lime; extreme low references are red. Each side is replaced by its next extreme event. The old fast/slow envelope alternatives are not specified fully in the guides.

The liquidity read comes first

  1. Locate: determine the parent channel direction and selected liquidity level. A colored volume bar away from a relevant area has little entry meaning.
  2. Compare: is reported participation increasing or drying? Is the bar wide, narrow, or closing far from its extreme? Same-time RVOL is only meaningful when prior sessions are actually supplied.
  3. Sequence: an accelerating bar followed by dense churn and rejection can support a stall hypothesis. Persistent closes through the level support acceptance instead.
  4. Wait: two subsequent bars are required for the follow-through assessment. Neither a wick nor failure to extend proves reversal. Timestamp a divergence only after its right-side confirmation.
  5. Review: volume supplements Taper's existing level watch. Keep the selected level, frozen stop ceiling, minimum reward/risk, source freshness and human paper-ticket confirmation.

Taper's model classifications describe supplied evidence. Their distributions are not win rates, target-hit probabilities or execution permissions.

What the 2015 CL examples teach

These are selected historical illustrations, not a controlled performance study, and they do not establish transfer to MYM or crypto.

Resistance: activity without progress

August 3 entry, pages 4–10: channel and Fib context frame a corrective upswing. Increasing activity with a narrowing range and close off the highs supports watching a rejection. The lower high and later price move supply confirmation.

Taper asks: did price reject the hunted upper level, or is it accepting through it despite churn?

Support: climax followed by a stall

August 3 exit, pages 12–17: downward acceleration reaches a channel/Fib area. The next dense bar closes away from the low. This supports reassessing downside progress; the colored bar alone does not establish a new uptrend.

Taper asks: is an opposite close observed, continuation observed, or is the sequence unresolved?

A corrective leg loses participation

August 5 entry, pages 29–32: the location is established first. A low-participation down leg then gains a completed upward response. The useful relationship is the sequence and location, rather than a rule to buy every low-volume candle.

Taper asks: does the resumption agree with parent context and an eligible selected level?

News and contract context matter

Pages 26 and 39–42 discuss a crude report, higher-frame geometry and fast reversal behavior. Tick-chart volume speed cannot be recovered from one-minute OHLCV. The examples mix dated and continuous contracts plus RTH and full sessions.

Taper asks: are event timing, contract identity and session comparison actually available?

Changing the chart view does not change Taper's frozen risk policy. Jev receives recomputed raw volume and available relative measurements together. Today's matched-window ratio is sum(volume) / sum(expected volume), not the average of individual RVOL percentages. A partial supplied window is not a full-session total.

1. Participation

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. How does recent reported volume compare with its SMA and, when adequately sampled, same-time historical RVOL? Name the available baseline; raw rolling average does not remove seasonality.

  • EXPANDING Increasing participation against available baseline.
  • CONTRACTING Decreasing participation against available baseline.
  • ORDINARY Near baseline without clear change.
  • MIXED Raw and same-time or recent comparisons disagree.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
2. Effort Result

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. Does high measured volume produce directional price progress, or little net progress with rejection? Small candle body alone is not absorption proof.

  • PROGRESS_UP Effort accompanies completed upward progress.
  • PROGRESS_DOWN Effort accompanies completed downward progress.
  • POSSIBLE_ABSORPTION High effort, limited progress and observed rejection at a relevant level.
  • ORDINARY No qualified effort/result anomaly.
  • MIXED Effort and result conflict without a resolved interpretation.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
3. Location

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. Where are recent climax/churn/spikes relative to selected liquidity and active channel/Fib context? Require actual contact and completed close, not merely a large colored bar.

  • UPPER_REJECTION Upper hunted level contact and close away below.
  • LOWER_REJECTION Lower hunted level contact and close away above.
  • BREAKOUT_ACCEPTANCE Observed consecutive closes accepting through a selected level.
  • NO_OVERLAP Available signal evidence is away from selected levels.
  • CONFLICTED Both sides or channels give incompatible contacts.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
4. Sequence

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. Assess the observed climax then churn sequence and candle close location; do not assume every sequence causes a turn.

  • UPPER_STALL Sequence with completed upper level rejection.
  • LOWER_STALL Sequence with completed lower level rejection.
  • CONTINUATION Sequence followed by completed acceptance in original direction.
  • NONE No qualified sequence in supplied recent evidence.
  • UNRESOLVED Sequence exists but subsequent evidence is inconclusive.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
5. Follow Through

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. What do timestamped followThrough states show for the most relevant recent signal? Never classify missing continuation as reversal. Use knownAt and no future bar.

  • CONTINUATION Two subsequent bars include a close beyond directional extreme.
  • OPPOSITE_BREAK Observed close beyond opposite extreme.
  • PENDING Two subsequent completed bars are not yet available.
  • UNRESOLVED No decisive close beyond either extreme, or incompatible events.
  • NONE No recent signal to assess.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
6. Pullback

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. Within available parent trend and a selected level, is the corrective leg losing participation and then resuming price progress? Avoid counting presumed pullbacks or calling a third pullback unsafe without evidence.

  • UP_RESUMPTION Rising parent context, lighter pullback and completed upward resumption.
  • DOWN_RESUMPTION Falling parent context, lighter pullback and completed downward resumption.
  • CORRECTION_ONLY Lighter corrective leg but no completed resumption.
  • CONFLICTED Volume or parent trend contradicts resumption.
  • NONE No coherent corrective leg and resumption evidence.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
7. Divergence

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. Assess confirmed raw-volume/price pivot divergences with recorded anchorTime and confirmedAt. Candle-sign delta cannot establish bid/ask divergence.

  • BEARISH Confirmed higher high with at least 20% lower raw pivot volume.
  • BULLISH Confirmed lower low with at least 20% lower raw pivot volume.
  • BOTH Recent confirmed divergences in both directions.
  • NONE No confirmed divergence; unconfirmed pivots do not qualify.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
8. Geometry Agreement

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. Does observed volume and price reaction agree with active higher-frame geometry? Broken rails are references; missing parents cannot establish agreement.

  • UP_ALIGNED Available parent trend and reaction support upward scenario.
  • DOWN_ALIGNED Available parent trend and reaction support downward scenario.
  • COUNTERTREND Reaction opposes a coherent parent trend.
  • CONFLICTED Parents and reaction disagree without coherent context.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
9. Invalidation

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. What observed evidence would invalidate the volume-supported reaction at the selected level? Assess actual closes and known signal extremes; invent no stop/target.

  • ACCEPTED_THROUGH Completed consecutive closes have accepted through the reaction level.
  • OPPOSITE_PROGRESS Observed strong directional progress contradicts the reaction.
  • STILL_CONDITIONAL Potential reaction has not been invalidated but remains conditional.
  • NO_VALID_REACTION No observed volume-supported reaction to invalidate.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
10. Supplement

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. How should volume supplement the selected-level liquidity watch? Use observed price confirmation and frozen risk gates directly, without seeing other question answers. Volume cannot create a level, move brackets, authorize a ticket or estimate profit.

  • SUPPORTS_LONG_WATCH Qualified lower reaction with compatible volume and price confirmation.
  • SUPPORTS_SHORT_WATCH Qualified upper reaction with compatible volume and price confirmation.
  • WAIT_CONFIRMATION Interesting volume but missing qualified completed confirmation.
  • CONTRADICTS Observed acceptance/progress opposes the proposed reaction.
  • NEUTRAL Available volume adds no meaningful directional evidence.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
11. Evidence Quality

Use `volumeEvidence` and completed context; apply its semantics. Independent question. Assess volume evidence quality, supplied history coverage, source freshness, missing bars, units and same-time reference counts. Need not have RVOL for a valid raw-volume read; report relative limitations explicitly.

  • RAW_ONLY Valid completed raw OHLCV; same-time sample unavailable.
  • RELATIVE_LIMITED Raw valid; same-time ratio has 3–14 observed historical samples.
  • RELATIVE_FULL Raw valid; same-time ratio has 15 prior observed samples.
  • CONFLICTED Volume source or measurements conflict.
  • UNAVAILABLE Required evidence missing or invalid.
