FRAMEWORK / FOUNDATION

How an indicator earns a new score

A candidate change is measured against frozen rules and a held out sample. Results carry the source, instrument, timeframe, cutoff, sample size, and uncertainty. A score advances in five point display steps only after an independent review accepts the evidence.

  1. 01 · DefineVersion the rule and outcome before testing.
  2. 02 · MeasureRecord resolved paper or shadow outcomes with provenance.
  3. 03 · CompareTest a candidate against the prior version on held out data.
  4. 04 · ReviewApprove the evidence before promoting a score or rule.

The Indicator Agent workbench now records protocols, paired observations, and descriptive scorecards. Data collection, Scientist dispatch, chart-code promotion, and unattended candidate evaluation are not automated. No score represents live trading performance.
