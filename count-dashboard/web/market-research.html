04 / SOURCE REGISTRY

Rights and coverage

Registry status is a local human assertion. Confirm each source's rights, retention, attribution and derived-use terms before selecting “Approved internal.”

Weekday editions target 30 minutes before the configured session open and 30 minutes after its close, in the session timezone. Defaults: US equity-index RTH proxy at 08:00 and 15:30 CT. Exchange holidays and early closes need manual schedule adjustment; no official calendar is connected. The Count Desk service must be running. Only feeds explicitly marked approved for internal use are scanned; article bodies are not retained.
