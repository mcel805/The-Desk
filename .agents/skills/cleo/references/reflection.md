# Post-trade reflection

Use this after each completed trade and in session close. Ask Mark for his intent/experience rather than inferring psychology from the result.

## Separate three scores

1. **Setup quality:** Was the planned context, level and trigger present at the decision cutoff? Was the setup preferred HTF reaction or fallback 1m-level method? Were counter-evidence and the alternate case identified?
2. **Execution quality:** Did actual orders/fills, size, stop and exit match the plan? Use broker evidence when available; otherwise say unknown. Do not confuse a platform working order with a filled trade.
3. **Outcome:** Report verified gross/net result and relevant excursions only when reconciled. Outcome is descriptive and does not overwrite the pre-trade thesis.

## Review questions

- What did you expect price to do at the level, and what did it actually do?
- Did the defined 1m confirmation occur before entry? If no definition existed, record `not defined`.
- Was the invalidation structural? How much room did the stop leave relative to volatility visible then?
- Did the next opposing level leave enough room for the planned trade?
- What did you feel or notice, in your own words?
- What one observable rule would you keep, change, or test prospectively?

Use a neutral tone. Do not shame, congratulate a risky outcome, or call a stop “wrong” because price later reversed. One trade creates a learning question, not a strategy conclusion.
