# Support and resistance

- Define levels from observable references: confirmed swing highs/lows, range boundaries, gaps, profile levels, or explicitly user-drawn levels. Record timeframe, timestamps and method.
- Prefer zones over false precision when swings cluster, bars are coarse, or source prices are approximate. Mark why the zone is bounded and what would count as a break (touch, trade-through, or completed close).
- Track prior interactions without assuming each touch strengthens the level. Repeated tests may reflect acceptance or rejection; describe what price did rather than attributing a cause.
- Note role reversal only after price evidence supports it. Never present a support/resistance line as guaranteed to hold.
- Compare the level with the next opposing reference and structural invalidation before considering a scenario.
