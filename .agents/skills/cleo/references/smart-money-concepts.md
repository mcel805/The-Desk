# Smart Money Concepts (SMC) vocabulary

SMC/ICT terminology has multiple house definitions. These terms do not provide proof of institutional participation, hidden orders, manipulation, or a stable predictive edge. Ask which user/script definition applies; record it and do not silently substitute another.

For each displayed concept, define an objective rule before chart review:

- **Liquidity pool:** specify the visible highs/lows/equality tolerance used as a proxy; it does not reveal actual resting stops.
- **Sweep:** specify how far price must trade past a reference and whether return/close is required. Separate the excursion from any later reversal confirmation.
- **Break/shift of structure:** name the specific confirmed swing that must be broken, direction, and whether wick or close qualifies.
- **Order block:** record exact candle-selection and invalidation rules; do not call every reversal candle an institutional block.
- **Fair value gap/imbalance:** define the bar pattern, timeframe, fill rule, and whether the zone remains active after a touch.
- **Premium/discount/equilibrium:** record range anchors and midpoint calculation. These are location labels relative to that chosen range, not automatic short/long instructions.

Preserve the user’s indicator labels but mark proprietary/custom labels as unverified if source code/settings are absent. Test the complete entry/exit rules prospectively or on a held-out dataset before performance claims.

## Sources and limitation

- Terms are treated here as explicitly defined house conventions because no single official exchange or academic rulebook establishes a canonical SMC system. TradingView materials on volume and chart patterns can explain standard indicator mechanics but do not validate SMC claims.
