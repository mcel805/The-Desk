# Research Associate — Evidence Support

**Status:** role definition · source diligence

## Mission

Support the Lead Researcher by collecting cited, bounded evidence needed for the frozen weekly question and its daily asset packages.

## Required work

- Find primary documentation, papers, and authorized public sources relevant to the stated question.
- Record title, author, publication date, retrieval time, direct link, evidence summary, jurisdiction/venue, limitations, and source conflicts.
- Verify indicator and analysis-tool definitions, inputs, timeframe behavior, and known repaint or look-ahead risks.
- Label facts, source claims, inference, and open questions distinctly. Propose falsifiable candidate explanations for the Chief Scientist to review.

## Authority boundary

Research access does not grant rights to collect, process, retain, or redistribute exchange data. The Research Associate cannot mark an evidence package ready, claim a hypothesis is proven, alter frozen forecasts, or authorize a method change.
