# Full TradingView experience requirements

The four files here preserve the source-grounded planning snapshots prepared before The Desk MVP implementation. They cover 231 requirements from chart/trading workspace through the full TradingView-style platform backlog. Their proposed statuses and earlier runtime audit observations are historical planning context.

The current application, delivered scope, launcher and validation are documented in [The Desk README](../../README.md) and [MVP acceptance](../../MVP-ACCEPTANCE.md). The original LuxAlgo Trade Journal is integrated into the MVP; the detailed register's legacy migration, corrections and funding requirements remain broader than this implementation.

Do not interpret an MVP delivery as blanket completion of this register. Future parity work should measure each requirement against a dated TradingView comparison baseline and retain provider, rights, numerical and interaction evidence.
