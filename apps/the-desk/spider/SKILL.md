## Market read

1. Establish provider, symbol/contract, timezone, session/date, accessible frames, freshness and completed-bar cutoff. Separate forming candles. Use identified chart/data tools or supplied images. Ask for the market if none can be identified; do not invent absent frames, prices or a feed.
2. Start weekly/daily when available, then 4H/1H for the parent draw; use 30m/15m/5m/1m as needed for clarity. Assign each frame a context, setup or execution role, preserving its own anchors; roles may share a frame. Higher-frame entries are valid when legible; refinement needs its own qualified smaller block and pool. Do not descend to manufacture entries when parent direction is unresolved. Label missing context; repeated views of the same swing are not independent confirmations.
3. Map meaningful pools on both sides, tracing reactions to their left-side source. Record reference, respect/departure, contact, sweep and rebuilding. Daily extremes are references, not automatic reversal signals. Identify obstacle, management pool and farther draw. Fresh sweep-origin extremes are only conditionally protected; OHLC cannot prove absence of orders.
4. Track the ordered participant hypothesis with actual swing/time/price anchors. BOS, momentum, imbalance or a block alone is insufficient. Identify who may have entered, what adverse excursion clears them and what opposing draw remains. Preserve a competing path. Scope every induced/trapped label to a timeframe and sequence; a local label does not automatically reverse the parent thesis.
5. Choose the active pool, not automatically the deepest old extreme. Newer lessons allow a fresh local inducement/trap while an older untouched origin remains. Require actual local formation and usable block/target support; retain older pools as competing/future references. After a genuine buyer trap, default against NEW shorts merely chasing an older low; mirror after a seller trap against NEW longs. This is not automatic reversal or exit. If conflicts remain, wait for a fresh continuation or stay out.
6. Choose one of two setup families: existing-block entry on the relevant secondary pool run, or confirmation that first builds a new sweep-and-departure block, new secondary liquidity, then the entry run. A first reaction/BOS alone is not confirmation. Existing-block entries need not wait for a new reversal candle. Alert, limit and market are execution methods, not additional families.
7. Apply the rulebook's evidence, parent, participant, block, objective, execution and management gates. A directional view can pass while its entry remains waiting or unavailable. Freeze entry/zone, full LB stop anchor, buffer/cost assumptions, technical targets, management event, cancellation and review event before calling it ready. Obtain missing paper budget/instrument value only if sizing is requested. Do not tighten inside a block to force R, widen a frozen stop or size additions without aggregate capacity. The source's personal ~3R preference is not Mark's default. Target actual liquidity, not arbitrary R multiples.
8. Apply the selected session/calendar plan. The source favors new entries after scheduled major news, avoids relevant bank holidays and mostly trades indices in NY, often after 09:30 cash open; earlier index and Asia FX exceptions are documented. Verify date-specific New York timezone, provider candle alignment and actual calendar for current actionable plans. H4/10 a.m., inside-day, candle-sequence, ping-pong and stacking models are conditional variants.
9. Use states: watch, waiting-for-trap, waiting-for-block, waiting-for-reaction, waiting-for-revisit, ready-paper, active-paper, missed, invalidated, completed-paper, insufficient-evidence. A ready anticipatory plan may await contact; confirmation cannot be ready before its conditions exist. Distinguish staged plan, contact, source-reported execution, assumed paper fill and verified fill. A chart arrow, trade tool or filename is not fill proof. Target consumed before entry cancels that attempt; rebuilding may support a new one.
10. Manage by named meaningful events, not green P&L or the first small reaction. A second trap can occur inside the original block. After the chosen pool is taken, apply the predefined partial/BE/structural roll. Partial percentages vary; never silently impose 25/50/75% or fixed BE. State residual risk/costs. Preserve losses, unfilled plans, revisions and separate re-entry attempts. A remote intact target does not override a failed stop or contradictory evidence.

## Mark and deliver

Deliver an actual marked artifact when requested. Use platform drawing controls or a source-preserving SVG/HTML overlay; do not generate replacement candles. For screenshots preserve originals/hashes and pixel anchors; label prices approximate. Cover each requested frame or label unavailable. Inspect anchors, overlaps and the visible artifact.

Keep originating sweep, block extreme, secondary pool, trigger, stop, management and final target separate. Use blue objectives, light block/imbalance zones, amber/red trap hypotheses, red stop/invalidation and dashed conditional paths; these are Spider conventions. Retain drawing IDs, timeframe, time/price or pixels, observed/conditional state and cutoff. Distinguish parent invalidation from the child stop.

Lead with status and next event. Include a compact timeframe map, ordered trap sequence, selected family, entry/stop/targets/management/cancellation, competing path and actual chart link. State material gaps once. High probability remains qualitative; headlines, P&L claims, repeated examples and frame agreement do not supply calibrated odds. Historical reads must respect the cutoff.
