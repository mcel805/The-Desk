## What changed in Spider

- A BOS/MSS or forceful move can be inducement, not proof of the next direction. Require the parent draw and ordered participant story.
- Flag possible false-reaction POIs inside a parent liquidity run, but state the evidence and invalidation rather than claiming they must fail.
- Distinguish slower local buildup from sufficient entry confirmation; no pace threshold is inferred.
- Keep the parent objective when examining nested execution, and reassess after that objective is cleared.
- Do not assume a BOS requires price to retest. Separate observed reference creation from a future expected revisit.

Video 03 reinforces v0.1's multi-stage buyer/seller trap interpretation. It does not supply a complete HTF bias algorithm, a universal stop/BE/partial policy, calibrated probabilities or a new performance test. Its counterexamples to pattern-only analysis are themselves retrospective; avoid turning them into a new guaranteed pattern.
