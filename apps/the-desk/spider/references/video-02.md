## What changed in Spider

- Favor POIs with a source-backed qualifying sweep and a separate secondary pool/trap. Do not treat every structural high/low as equally qualified.
- Preserve direct POI entry as an explicit anticipatory family alongside lower-timeframe confirmed entries. Distinguish a prepared limit from actual contact/fill.
- Mark the original sweep, POI boundary, secondary pool, later revisit and opposing objective separately.
- Assess liquidity outside the selected structural stop, not only attractive reward inside the intended direction.

The presenter's “liquidity block” terminology gains a sweep/POI association, but its exact candle/body/wick boundary remains unformalized. No new partial-size, BE, risk, expiry or performance rule was established.
