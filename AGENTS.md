# Trading Research repository guidance

- This repository is the Trading Research domain in the Digital Company workspace.
- Treat chart-derived structures and model outputs as research hypotheses; label source, timeframe, cutoff, and uncertainty.
- Keep all trading behavior paper/shadow only. No live order routing, account control, or claims of live market access without an explicitly approved and verified integration.
- Preserve provenance for market data, charts, indicators, and derived reports. Do not invent missing prices, timestamps, fees, or contract details.
- Keep credentials, local databases, logs, and private account/company records out of Git.
- Generated `tmp/`, `output/`, and `.lavish/` material is preserved locally but excluded from the baseline until individually curated.
- Firstmate owns task lifecycle when connected. A specialist prompt or dashboard badge does not imply a dispatched worker.
