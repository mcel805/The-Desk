# Initial chart-markup study

September 10, 2026. Original study notes from public primary sources. Textual method review completed for the entries below; image references are not a claim of completed visual chart inspection. Full books and subscriber archives remain unstudied.

## Prechter / EWI tradition

[Waveopedia](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/) explains nesting and primary/alternate interpretations. Adopt explicit degree keys and conditional invalidation. Do not treat a valid count as a unique outcome. This is institutional educational material, not a verified personal chart sample by Prechter.

[Channeling](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/channeling/) gives concrete drawing anchors: after wave 3, connect 1 and 3 with a parallel through 2; after wave 4, connect 2 and 4 and consider the parallel through 3, or through 1 for a very strong third wave. These are target guides, not mandatory endpoints. Keep anchor IDs in the ledger.

[Impulse](https://www.elliottwave.com/waveopedia/impulse/) distinguishes rules from guidelines and addresses cash-market overlap versus rare futures extremes. For the baseline, flag overlap and investigate the instrument and source qualification; never use a vague futures exception as an automatic pass. Diagonals need their own rule set.

## Neely

[Plotting data, January 23, 2006](https://www.neowave.com/qow/qow-archive-29.asp): high/low sequencing matters. [Spacing, December 27, 2006](https://www.neowave.com/qow/qow-archive-137.asp): his Wave charts use ordered extrema with equal spacing. These Q&A texts were retrieved in search. Daily OHLC alone cannot identify whether the high or low came first; fetch intrabar evidence or mark ordering unresolved.

[What is NEoWave?](https://www.neowave.com/what-is-neowave.asp) was opened and identifies the specialized Wave-chart representation and links example charts. It does not establish superiority through independent testing. [March 2023 timeframe correction](https://www.neowave.com/qow/qow-archive-5239.asp), read in the initial package research, takes precedence over attributing a sub-daily workflow to Neely.

Agent adaptation: use his concern for data construction and logical testing; only call a chart NEoWave when its data and method requirements are met. Preserve ordinary raw price charts and any derived representation separately.

## Hochberg / Short Term Update

[A Wave Counting Shortcut](https://www.elliottwave.com/wave-riffs/a-wave-counting-shortcut/) was opened. The STU excerpt uses total visible subdivisions as a preliminary impulse/correction screen, with a triangle qualification. Treat this as a screening aid, not a replacement for checking each pattern rule. The linked chart image could not be fetched in this pass. The example is a 90-minute range chart, not evidence of Daily-specific efficacy.

[Hochberg's profile](https://www.elliottwave.com/bio/steven-hochberg/) identifies his STU role. The article is attributed here to the service; individual authorship is not established on that page. Adopt concise structural updates and versioned changes, not an invented personal chart style.
