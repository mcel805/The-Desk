# Literature-based training curriculum

Objective: develop inspectable subject knowledge and disciplined application. The stages below are proposed training assignments, not completed work or validated learning thresholds.

## Module 1 — Language and provenance

Read EW01 from an authorized copy and EW02 for context. Build a glossary of wave, degree, impulse, correction, extension, truncation, diagonal, zigzag, flat, triangle, combination, alternation, and invalidation. Every definition needs a locator and its methodological school. Separate mandatory constraints from tendencies. Deliver 20 reviewed cards and five original diagrams with counterexamples. Do not infer definitions solely from book descriptions.

## Module 2 — Traditional pattern reasoning

Study EW01 pattern sections. Explain which observations support or reject each candidate, and which subdivisions cannot be resolved at Daily resolution. Deliver ten masked chart exercises, including invalid impulses and ambiguous corrections. The agent must be able to reject an attractive but invalid count and explain why.

## Module 3 — Neely comparison

Read NW01–NW05 and the authorized full Mastering Elliott Wave. Study its data construction and pattern-validation requirements from the source before application. Produce a side-by-side comparison with Module 2: same term, shared requirements, additional requirements, exceptions, and disagreements. Include NW05's later timeframe correction and its publication date. Do not teach a 4H workflow as Neely's recommendation.

## Module 4 — Goodburn and measurable structure

Study WT01 and WT02, then authorized examples from WT03–WT04. Recalculate ratio projections from stated anchors. Explain when different projections converge and when choosing anchors after the outcome creates hindsight bias. Deliver five original worksheets showing invalidation and conditions required before a projected reversal can be considered supported.

## Module 5 — Forecasts as dated arguments

Study authorized EW04–EW05, NW06, and WT04 issues. For each, record what was knowable at publication: instrument, data cutoff, count, alternatives, target, invalidation, and deadline if specified. Label missing conditions unscorable rather than inventing them. Include failed and revised calls. Compare reasoning discipline; do not rank authors from selected promotional archives.

## Module 6 — Blind Daily/4H application

Use historical chart windows whose later bars are hidden. Create a full Daily count with weekly context and separately scoped 4H refinement. Freeze the report before revealing the outcome. Score rule adherence independently of direction: a valid count can fail and an invalid count can accidentally predict direction.

## Module 7 — Review and durable knowledge

Mark reviews cards and exercise reports. Keep approved, rejected, and pending records separate. Store corrections as new versions with reasons. Future sessions retrieve approved cards and their original source excerpts. Do not treat model-generated text as independent corroboration.

## Suggested initial corpus

Aim for 60–100 concise reviewed knowledge cards and 50 development chart cases before a first held-out assessment. These are practical starting quantities, not a guarantee of capability. Include clean trends, sideways periods, gaps, conflicting counts, missing data, invalid patterns, and futures roll artifacts. More examples of a single easy pattern will not cover the specialty.

Each card should answer: What does the author say? What conditions apply? What would be a counterexample? How does another school differ? What can the data actually establish? Cite sources, paraphrase originally, and preserve access/rights metadata.

## Optional actual weight training

Only after prompt plus retrieval establishes a baseline, identify repeated behavior failures that examples could address. Prepare expert-reviewed input/output pairs and preferred/rejected report pairs, not a bulk dump of books. Separate chart periods across development, validation, and final test; group overlapping windows to prevent leakage. Never include future outcome text in the input.

Verify current fine-tuning model eligibility, image support, licensing, and data handling before a job is created. No training job or billable run is part of this package. Compare any tuned model against the unchanged retrieval baseline on the held-out set. Adopt it only if measured gains justify added cost and maintenance.
