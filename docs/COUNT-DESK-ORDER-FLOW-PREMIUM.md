# Quant Lab premium order flow

The Premium drawer offers Volume Profile, TPO, VWAP, Footprint, and Volume Bubbles as descriptive chart studies. The first three use completed public OHLCV bars. Profile volume at price is an OHLCV range-overlap **estimate**, TPO counts touched time blocks rather than traded volume, and VWAP weights HLC3 by reported candle volume. None is a directional probability or an order instruction.

On a Coinbase Exchange chart, Footprint and Bubbles use the latest candle that was complete when the chart snapshot was requested. The bounded, client-memory-only adapter verifies the product ID and quote increment, fetches newest-first trade pages until the entire candle is covered, rejects malformed or repeated executions, and reconciles summed trade size with the corresponding Coinbase candle within 0.01%. It never fills missing volume. Coinbase documents trade `side` as the maker side, so a maker sell is counted as aggressive buy/ask volume and a maker buy as aggressive sell/bid volume. The Footprint ladder shows bid and ask size at each exact trade price; Bubbles displays larger executed-price clusters. Chart marks may group adjacent ticks to remain visible at the current zoom.

The adapter is limited to 25 trade pages. A market or bar that exceeds coverage, has missing/invalid trade fields, or fails volume reconciliation reports unavailable. Binance and Hyperliquid chart adapters remain candle-only for these studies. The feature is scoped to local personal or internal research. The [Coinbase Market Data Terms](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/market_data) restrict redistribution and external end-user use; this implementation does not grant those rights. Trades are not persisted or used for order routing.

Sources: [Coinbase Exchange trades](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-product-trades), [trade pagination](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/exchange/rest-api/pagination), [product metadata](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-single-product), [market-data terms](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/market_data).

Verification: `node count-dashboard/web/test-order-flow-premium.mjs`, `node count-dashboard/web/test-quant-order-flow-overlays.mjs`, and `node count-dashboard/web/test-coinbase-footprint-feed.mjs`. Browser verification on a Coinbase BTC-USD 1m chart showed both studies active on one completed bar after 217 trades reconciled with its candle; the exact-price ladder and Bubbles tab rendered.
