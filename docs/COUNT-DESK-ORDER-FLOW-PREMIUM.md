# Count Desk premium order flow, v1

## Product choice

Volume Profile, TPO, VWAP, Footprint, and Volume Bubbles are premium **indicators** in the Tool Belt and Chart Workspace. They overlay candles. Quant Lab's separate Premium drawer also lists all five. Volume Profile, TPO, and VWAP run on completed public bars. On a Coinbase Exchange chart, Footprint and Bubbles query public executions for the latest bar that was complete when the chart snapshot was requested. They draw only after trade pagination covers that bar and executed volume reconciles with its Coinbase candle. Binance and Hyperliquid chart adapters remain candle-only for these studies. Quant Lab's compact TPO draws time-block counts as a profile width because row letters collide in narrow chart layouts. A dedicated Footprint or TPO chart type would need native renderer and data-provider work; it is not represented as present here. The visual ideas come from the [LuxAlgo order flow introduction](https://docs.luxalgo.com/platform/charts/order-flow/introduction), [Volume Profiles](https://docs.luxalgo.com/platform/charts/order-flow/volume-profiles), [TPO](https://docs.luxalgo.com/platform/charts/order-flow/tpo), [Footprint](https://docs.luxalgo.com/platform/charts/order-flow/footprint), [VWAP](https://docs.luxalgo.com/platform/charts/order-flow/vwap), and [Volume Bubbles](https://docs.luxalgo.com/platform/charts/order-flow/volume-bubbles). This is an independent Count Desk implementation, not LuxAlgo source or a parity claim.

## Data and calculation contract

| Study | Input | Calculation | Current availability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Volume Profile | Closed OHLCV bars | Reported bar volume allocated to price rows in proportion to intersection length between bar range and row. A zero-range bar goes to its price row. Rows conserve the sum of reported volume. Highest row is POC; 70% value area expands from POC into the adjacent row with more volume. | Chart and Tool Belt workbench; **estimated** volume at price. Rolling bars or UTC day session on chart. |
| TPO | Closed OHLC bars | One letter per UTC day block at each row touched by the block's high-low range. POC counts letters; 70% value area counts letters, not volume. | Chart overlay; block size must be at least chart bar spacing. This is OHLC range presence, not tick-level time at price. |
| VWAP | Closed OHLCV bars | Cumulative sum of source price × reported volume divided by cumulative reported volume; source is HLC3, OHLC4, or close. Bands use the same volume-weighted population standard deviation. Accumulators reset on UTC day, week, or month boundary. | Chart overlay. Missing volume is never replaced with a fabricated unit. |
| Footprint | Executed volume by bar, tick price, and aggressor side | Bid-side sell volume and ask-side buy volume are aggregated at verified tick size; delta = ask − bid. | Quant Lab: recent, completed Coinbase Exchange bar after a full-window and candle-volume check. Tool Belt/Chart Workspace still need an execution-capable provider. |
| Volume Bubbles | Verified footprint rows | Bubble size uses executed total volume at bar × price; filter is relative to the largest available cluster. | Same Coinbase Quant Lab gate and Chart Workspace limitation as Footprint. |

The Chart Workspace footprint adapter accepts only a provider `getFootprints()` payload with `authority: executed_trades`, `sideConvention: aggressor_at_bid_ask`, `complete: true`, a named source, units, tick size, and closed bar-aligned timestamps. Its current provider interface does not supply this. Quant Lab instead has a bounded, client-memory-only Coinbase Exchange trade adapter. It verifies product identity and quote increment, fetches newest-first trade pages until a timestamp earlier than the chosen bar, rejects repeated or malformed executions, and sums exact base-asset size by price and aggressor side. Coinbase documents `side` as the **maker** side: maker sell is aggressive buy/ask volume; maker buy is aggressive sell/bid volume. The executed-size total must match the corresponding Coinbase OHLCV bar within 0.01% or the study reports unavailable. Chart marks merge adjacent ticks into labeled price buckets when needed for zoom; the on-chart ladder shows Footprint bid/ask sizes at each exact trade tick and a separate Bubbles cluster table. The API request is capped at 25 trade pages, so active markets and older or longer bars can be unavailable. [Coinbase trades](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-product-trades), [pagination](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/exchange/rest-api/pagination), [product metadata](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-single-product).

The [Coinbase Market Data Terms](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/market_data) allow personal or internal research use subject to their conditions and restrict redistribution and external end-user use. This local Quant Lab feature is scoped to that research use; it is not a grant to publish or redistribute the feed. It does not persist trades or route orders. A hosted or externally shared product would require a separate rights review.

The [Tickstream reference](https://tick-stream.xyz/llms-full.txt) describes exchange aggressor side on its futures tick channel, but its published reference also says a separate license is required when users see redistributed data. Count Desk currently discards side when it builds bounded OHLCV bars. A configured API key and a connected socket do not establish order-flow display, processing, retention, or redistribution approval. No such approval record, dated-contract mapping, or verified tick-size source is recorded for a futures footprint integration.

Source, symbol, timeframe, cutoff, missing-volume count, and calculation method appear in the chart status for candle studies. UTC session boundaries are deliberate v1 limits and are not exchange RTH/ETH calendars. All studies are descriptive and paper/shadow only; no order execution or predictive probability is implied.

The chart studies use confirmed historical bars. Count Desk excludes its newest stored bar because a later stored bar is its close confirmation; direct chart providers use the chart's snapshot-request time as the completion cutoff. A candle that was forming when fetched never becomes eligible merely because the page remains open. Missing reported volume creates a partial profile or a VWAP gap; zero reported volume contributes zero weight. VWAP starts a new accumulator on the chosen UTC anchor; TPO needs a chart interval no wider than its block interval. Replay/reload must recalculate from the same closed-bar prefix and settings, without future bars.

## Verification

`node count-dashboard/web/test-order-flow-premium.mjs` checks volume conservation, price gaps, missing-volume handling, UTC VWAP reset and deviation, causal prefix values, TPO block counts and higher-timeframe rejection, and exact footprint aggregation. `python3 -m unittest test_volume_profile_lab` from `count-dashboard` checks the server-side Volume Profile workbench and cutoff-safe report path. Browser verification checks premium cards, chart rendering, and the data gate for Footprint and Bubbles.

`node count-dashboard/web/test-quant-order-flow-overlays.mjs` checks Quant Lab's catalog, actual drawing output from the shared calculation reports, cutoff bounds, the fixed snapshot cutoff, and the execution-feed gate. `node count-dashboard/web/test-coinbase-footprint-feed.mjs` checks maker-side inversion, duplicate and malformed data rejection, full-window pagination, candle-volume reconciliation, and display bucket volume conservation. In the local Quant Lab browser, all six Premium cards (including Liquidity) appeared; Volume Profile, TPO, and VWAP each activated on a Binance BTCUSDT 5m public-bar snapshot. Footprint and Bubbles both activated on the same Coinbase BTC-USD 1m snapshot after 135 executions reconciled with the candle; the exact-price ladder and Bubbles tab rendered in the chart. A stale-snapshot attempt on a still-forming candle was rejected before the cutoff fix.
